Ludwigia sedioides

Ludwigia sedioides

Common Names: Mosaic Plant, Mosaic Flower, False Loosestrife

Local Name: மொசைக் செடி (Mosaic Chedi)

Family: Onagraceae

Origin: Brazil, South America

Plant Type: Floating-leaf aquatic perennial

Variety: Ludwigia sedioides (single species)

Average Lifespan: Perennial in tropical climates

🌿 Botanical Description
Growth HabitFloating rosette-forming aquatic; produces diamond-shaped leaves in a circular mosaic pattern on the water surface
Plant SizeRosettes 10–30 cm diameter; stems can reach 30–60 cm below water
LeavesSmall, diamond-shaped, arranged in a precise geometric mosaic pattern; red-green coloration
FlowersSmall yellow flowers emerge above the water surface
Root SystemSubmerged roots anchored in substrate; also produces adventitious roots
☀ Growing Conditions
ClimateTropical to subtropical; frost-sensitive
Temperature Range22–30°C ideal water temperature
Sun RequirementFull sun (high light required for mosaic pattern)
Water RequirementFully aquatic; needs still or slow-moving water 15–60 cm deep
Soil TypeRich aquatic substrate or pond soil
Soil pH Preference6.0–7.5
Can it Grow in Pots?Yes, in submerged pots or container water gardens
🌾 Cultivation & Care
Fertilizer ScheduleRoot tabs or liquid fertilizer monthly during growing season
Recommended Organic FertilizersAquatic plant root tabs, clay-based fertilizers
Common PestsAphids on emergent parts, snails
Common DiseasesAlgae overgrowth, leaf rot in poor water quality
Disease Prevention & RemediesMaintain clean water, adequate circulation, remove dead leaves promptly
🌸 Flowering & Fruiting
Flowering MonthsSummer months in warm conditions
Pollination TypeSelf-pollinating; insect-assisted
🌱 Pollination & Propagation
Propagation by SeedsSeeds can be collected from flowers; sow on wet substrate
Propagation by CuttingsStem cuttings root easily in water; division of rosettes
💧 Environmental Impact & Sustainability
Water UsageFully aquatic; provides shade reducing evaporation
Biodiversity BenefitsProvides shelter for fish fry and aquatic invertebrates; oxygenates water
