Java Moss

Taxiphyllum barbieri

Common Names: Java Moss

Local Name: —

Family: Hypnaceae

Origin: Southeast Asia

Plant Type: Aquatic moss

Variety: Moss (Submerged)

Average Lifespan: Perennial in tropical climates

🌿 Botanical Description
Growth HabitHardy aquatic moss that attaches to rocks and driftwood, sheltering fish fry
Plant SizeAquatic moss
LeavesOxygenating, Fish fry shelter, Very hardy, Low light OK
☀ Growing Conditions
ClimateTropical to subtropical; frost-sensitive
Sun RequirementLow-Med light
Water RequirementFully aquatic
Soil TypeAquatic substrate or pond soil
Soil pH Preference6.0–7.5
Can it Grow in Pots?Yes, in submerged containers or water gardens
🌾 Cultivation & Care
Fertilizer ScheduleRoot tabs or liquid fertilizer monthly during growing season
Recommended Organic FertilizersAquatic plant root tabs, clay-based fertilizers
Common PestsAlgae, snails, aphids on emergent parts
Common DiseasesLeaf rot, melting in poor water conditions
Disease Prevention & RemediesMaintain clean water, adequate light, remove dead leaves
🌱 Pollination & Propagation
Propagation by CuttingsStem cuttings or division; plant directly in substrate
💧 Environmental Impact & Sustainability
Water UsageFully aquatic; helps maintain water quality
Biodiversity BenefitsOxygenates water, provides shelter for aquatic life, absorbs excess nutrients
